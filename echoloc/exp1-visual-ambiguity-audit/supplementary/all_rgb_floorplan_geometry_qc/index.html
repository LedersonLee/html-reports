All-test RGB–floorplan geometry QC

This measures representation agreement, not model accuracy. At each stored test RGB/pose index, it compares source rendered scan depth with depth rendered from the current extruded floorplan proxy. A high discrepancy can reflect a pose/map-frame error, furniture/clutter, open/closed doors, scan holes, or an inadequate 2D map. It is a gate for excluding unreliable ambiguity examples, not proof of one specific cause.

Profile MAD is the median per-column absolute depth difference around the image centre. corr<0.2 means weak directional shape correspondence. severe requires both MAD>1 m and corr<0.2, avoiding a claim based on either metric alone.
